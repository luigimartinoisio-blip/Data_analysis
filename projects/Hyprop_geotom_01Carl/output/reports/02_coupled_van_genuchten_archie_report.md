## 1. Physical Theory and Formulation
The coupled hydrogeophysical model strictly binds the independent water retention parameters ($lpha_{	ext{VG}}, n_{	ext{VG}}, m_{	ext{VG}}$) from HYPROP2, resolving parameter equifinality down to a single physical degree of freedom ($n_A$):

$$	ext{EC}(h) = 	ext{EC}_{	ext{res}} + (	ext{EC}_{	ext{sat}} - 	ext{EC}_{	ext{res}}) \cdot \left[ 1 + (lpha_{	ext{VG}} \cdot h)^{n_{	ext{VG}}} ight]^{-m_{	ext{VG}} \cdot n_A}$$

where the compound exponent $\Gamma = m_{	ext{VG}} \cdot n_A$ governs the true physical slope, and $n_A = \Gamma \cdot \mathcal{K}(n_{	ext{VG}})$ reflects the apparent Archie saturation exponent amplified by the pore-geometry factor $\mathcal{K} = 1/m_{	ext{VG}}$.


## 2. Methodological Foundations of $\text{EC}_{\text{sat}}$: Physical Parallelism with $\theta_{\text{sat}}$

A critical theoretical question arises regarding the definition of saturated conductivity $\text{EC}_{\text{sat}}$ and why, in monodisperse clean sand (`Sand_R`), the fitted value ($\text{EC}_{\text{sat}} = 10.66\text{ mS/m}$) lies below the initial peak measurement ($\text{EC}_{\max} = 15.88\text{ mS/m}$ at $t=7\text{ h}$). Rather than an artifact, this divergence reflects a fundamental hydrophysical equivalence shared between the hydraulic van Genuchten formulation and our geoelectrical coupling.

### 2.1 Why $\theta_{\text{sat}}$ is Lower than Measured $\theta_{\max}$ (Hydraulic Domain)
In coarse, uniform sands, capillary saturation from the base fills both intergranular capillary pores and open boundary macropores. During the initial 28 hours of evaporation, matric suction remains virtually flat ($h < 3.65\text{ kPa}$, with $\partial h / \partial t \approx 0.03\text{ kPa/h}$), while approximately $10\%$ volumetric water evaporates freely:
- The initial measured water content on the HYPROP balance is $\theta(t=0) = 34.41\%$.
- However, standard soil physics software (**HYPROP-FIT**) treats $\theta_{\text{sat}}$ not as a static instantaneous reading, but as an asymptotic matrix parameter:
  $$\theta_{\text{sat}} \equiv \lim_{h \to 0} \theta(h) = 23.90\%$$
HYPROP-FIT deliberately discards the non-capillary gravitational excess ($34.4\% \to 23.9\%$) to capture the true capillary storage capacity of the matrix.

### 2.2 Why $\text{EC}_{\text{sat}}$ is Lower than Measured $\text{EC}_{\max}$ (Geoelectrical Domain)
In complete physical symmetry, the electrical conductivity during the first 28 hours reflects conduction through both the capillary matrix and the temporary gravitational water films. 
Because suction requires over 70 hours to reach the hydraulic Air Entry Point ($\psi_{\text{AEP}} = 5.48\text{ kPa}$), the experimental window before $\psi_{\text{AEP}}$ contains 71 measurements where conductivity gradually transitions from $15.88\text{ mS/m}$ down to $3.95\text{ mS/m}$.
Our universal **bounded leash** protocol defines:
$$\overline{\text{EC}}_{\text{pre-AEP}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{pre}}} \sum_{h_i \le \psi_{\text{AEP}}} \text{EC}(h_i) = 9.77\text{ mS/m}$$
$$\text{EC}_{\text{sat}} \in [0.95 \cdot \overline{\text{EC}}_{\text{pre-AEP}},\ 1.10 \cdot \overline{\text{EC}}_{\text{pre-AEP}}] = [9.21,\ 10.66]\text{ mS/m}$$
The non-linear optimization hits the upper ceiling of the leash at $\text{EC}_{\text{sat}} = 10.66\text{ mS/m}$, successfully filtering out the transient gravitational conduction and anchoring to the matrix baseline.
