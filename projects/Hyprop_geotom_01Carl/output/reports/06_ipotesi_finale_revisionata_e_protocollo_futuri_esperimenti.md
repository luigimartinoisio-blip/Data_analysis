## 2. The Core Scientific Hypothesis: Two Decoupled Controls

The dual-variable hydro-geoelectric hypothesis states that:

1. **Textural Scaling Control ($D_{EH}$)**:
   The **Electro-Hydraulic Decoupling Index** $D_{EH} = \frac{n_A}{m_{\mathrm{VG}}}$ expresses exclusively how the coupled exponent parameters scale against the **granulometric skeleton** (sand framework vs total fine matrix):
   $$D_{EH} = 2.05 \cdot 10^{0.0124 \cdot (\% \text{Fines})} \quad (r = +0.956)$$
   This governs the theoretical slope divergence between $S_e(h)$ and $\sigma_{\mathrm{norm}}(h)$.

2. **Mineralogical & Surface Buffering Control ($\text{GAP area}$)**:
   The actual geometric shape and integral area between the desaturation curves:
   $$\Delta_{\mathrm{GAP}} = \int_{\log_{10} h_{\min}}^{\log_{10} h_{\max}} \left[ S_e(\log_{10} h) - \sigma_{\mathrm{norm}}(\log_{10} h) \right] d(\log_{10} h)$$
   is **modulated by the presence and activity of expandable clay minerals (mixed-layer Illite/Smectite)**. Expandable smectite layers provide continuous surface conduction along the Electrical Double Layer (EDL), buffering bulk electrical conductivity and preventing it from plunging abruptly as capillary pore throats drain.

- **Why ML9 exhibits Maximum GAP ($\Delta_{\mathrm{GAP}} = 1.234\ \text{decades}$)**:
  ML9 is dominated by silt micro-pores ($57.3\%$) with low clay content ($26.2\%$). As suction builds, pore necks drain first, severing electrolytic connectivity in bulk water while pore bodies stay full. In the absence of a pervasive smectitic EDL, electrical conductivity collapses vertically.
- **Why ML10 exhibits Compressed GAP ($\Delta_{\mathrm{GAP}} = 1.018\ \text{decades}$)**:
  Although ML10 has **more clay ($33.0\%$)** and more than **double the phyllosilicates ($65.5\%$)** than ML9, its $\text{GAP area}$ is **smaller**. The expansive smectite network sustains a continuous ionic conduit along particle surfaces (EDL buffering), keeping relative conductivity higher across the capillary domain.
